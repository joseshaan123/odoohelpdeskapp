Experience the Odoo Helpdesk app

Try it yourself in three simple steps. First, click the colorful AI button at the top right and choose a suggestion such as "Summarize ticket", "Draft a reply" or "Set priority & tags". Next, click Use as reply, or press the ✨AI button in the message box, and the email writes itself using the details of the ticket. Finally, read it, click Send, and try the stage buttons, the stars and Close Ticket to see how a request moves through your team. This is a demonstration with sample data, so no real email is sent and nothing is saved.
